OpenAPI schema

      
Speech and dialogue API


GET /v1/models · GET /v1/audio/voices
Actual Qwen checkpoints, native speakers, compatibility aliases and saved voices.


POST /v1/audio/speech
OpenAI-compatible plain-text speech; MP3, WAV, FLAC, Opus, AAC, Ogg and raw PCM. Saved voices accept delivery instructions.


POST /tts/generate · POST /tts/stream · POST /tts/events
Complete audio, progressive audio or native SSE. Explicit text, SSML and SSML-H input modes.


POST /tts/plan · POST /tts/metrics
Validate and inspect the full synthesis plan before loading a model.


GET /tts/ssml/capabilities
Exact bounded SSML and SSML-H subset, descriptors and limits.


POST /tts/magic/take · /tts/magic/render · /tts/magic/assemble-upload
Preview/regenerate turns, reuse locked takes and assemble dialogue.


GET/POST /tts/voice-profiles · PUT/DELETE /tts/voice-profiles/{id}
Save, edit and use audio-backed voice references.


GET/POST /tts/dialogue-scripts · POST /tts/dialogue-workspaces
Scripts, folders, settings, takes and explicitly saved audio.


POST /tts/transcribe-upload · POST /tts/postprocess-upload
Configured external ASR and bounded FFmpeg audio effects.


POST /tts/purge · GET /system/gpu
Unload this Studio's model and inspect NVIDIA telemetry.


Raw PCM is 24 kHz mono signed 16-bit little endian. Browser streaming uses MP3. Built-in delivery direction requires 1.7B CustomVoice; Base direction is experimental. SSML audio and phonemes are unsupported. ASR runs externally.

SSML and SSML-H
Use a speak root. Supported: voice, lang, prosody, break, p, s, sub and bounded say-as. SSML-H adds designed characters and h:direction delivery instructions. Phonemes, remote audio and external lexicons are unsupported.
Rate, pitch and volume are audio effects. Explicit pauses replace the 120 ms turn gap, including 0ms. Exact capabilities and limits
